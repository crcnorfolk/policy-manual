These are the policies of CrossRoads Church in Norfolk, Virginia. They are public. Anyone can read them, link to them, or print them.

Every policy on this site shows four things at the top: the date it takes effect, the body that approved it, the date of that approval, and what earlier version it replaces. Each one also links to its full revision history, so you can see what changed and when.

## How to use this manual

Use the search box at the top to find a policy by subject. Policies are numbered, and a policy keeps its number for as long as it exists, so a link to a policy will keep working even when the manual is reorganized.

A policy marked **Draft** has been written but not approved, and is not in force. A policy marked **Retired** is no longer in force and is kept so the record stays complete.

## What this manual is not

The approval record is the elder board minutes. This site displays what the minutes say. Where the two ever disagree, the minutes govern.
